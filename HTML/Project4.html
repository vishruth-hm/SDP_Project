DISCOVER THE STATE

About Karnataka

A Land of Culture, Heritage and Nature

Karnataka is a beautiful state located in the southern part of India. It is known for its rich history, diverse culture, magnificent monuments, beautiful landscapes and traditional cuisine.

From the modern city of Bengaluru to the historic ruins of Hampi, Karnataka offers a unique combination of tradition and modernity.

The state has ancient temples, royal palaces, beaches, forests, waterfalls and hill stations. Every region of Karnataka has something different to offer to visitors.

01

Mysuru

STEP INTO THE PAST

Historical Places

Karnataka has a rich historical heritage represented by ancient temples, palaces, forts and monuments.

🏛️

Hampi

Hampi contains the remains of the Vijayanagara Empire. Temples, stone monuments and other structures spread across the landscape showcase the architectural heritage of the region.

🏰

Mysore Palace

Mysore Palace is a famous royal palace in Mysuru. Its grand architecture, decorated interiors and illuminated appearance attract many visitors.

🛕

Belur

Belur is known for the Chennakeshava Temple and its detailed stone carvings. The temple represents the artistic achievements of the Hoysala period.

🛕

Halebidu

Halebidu is famous for its Hoysaleswara Temple. The detailed sculptures and carvings make it an important historical and architectural destination.

⛰️

Badami Caves

The Badami cave temples are carved into sandstone cliffs. They contain sculptures and architectural features representing the region's ancient heritage.

🕌

Gol Gumbaz

Gol Gumbaz in Vijayapura is famous for its enormous dome and distinctive architectural design. It is one of the well-known monuments of Karnataka.

EXPERIENCE NATURE

Waterfalls & Hill Stations

Karnataka's natural beauty includes waterfalls, forests, mountains, coffee plantations and peaceful hill regions.

WATERFALL

Jog Falls

Jog Falls is one of Karnataka's most famous waterfalls. Water drops dramatically from a great height, creating an impressive natural spectacle during the suitable season.

WATERFALL

Abbey Falls

Abbey Falls is located near Madikeri in Coorg. Surrounded by greenery, the waterfall is a popular attraction for visitors exploring Kodagu.

HILL STATION

Coorg

Coorg is surrounded by green hills and coffee plantations. The region is known for its scenic landscapes, forests and pleasant environment.

HILL STATION

Chikmagalur

Chikmagalur is a beautiful hill region known for coffee estates, mountain landscapes and outdoor activities.

TASTE KARNATAKA

Famous Foods

Karnataka's cuisine varies across different regions. Traditional dishes combine local ingredients with distinctive flavours.

01

Bisi Bele Bath

A traditional rice-based dish prepared with lentils, vegetables and a special blend of spices. It is commonly served hot.

02

Mysore Pak

A popular sweet associated with Mysuru. It is made using gram flour, ghee and sugar and has a rich, soft texture.

03

Ragi Mudde

Ragi Mudde is a traditional Karnataka food prepared using ragi flour. It is commonly eaten with curries and other side dishes.

04

Neer Dosa

Neer Dosa is a thin and soft dosa made from rice batter. It is especially popular in coastal Karnataka.

05

Dharwad Peda

Dharwad Peda is a well-known Karnataka sweet with a distinctive caramelised flavour and soft texture.

06

Mangaluru Buns

Mangaluru buns are soft, slightly sweet fried breads traditionally prepared using fermented dough.

Travel Information

📅

Best Time to Visit

Karnataka can be explored in different seasons. The cooler months are generally comfortable for sightseeing and outdoor activities.

🚆

Transportation

Major cities and tourist destinations can be reached through road, rail and air connections. Local buses, taxis and other transport options can be used for sightseeing.

🏨

Accommodation

Hotels, resorts, homestays and budget accommodations are available in major tourist destinations. Choose accommodation according to your location and budget.

🎒

Travel Tips

  • Carry drinking water during outdoor trips.
  • Keep important documents safe.
  • Wear comfortable clothes and footwear.
  • Follow safety instructions at tourist places.
  • Keep natural and historical places clean.
  • Respect local culture and traditions.
