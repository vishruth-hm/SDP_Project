Welcome to Karnataka

Karnataka is a beautiful state in southern India known for its rich history, culture, natural beauty and delicious food. From ancient monuments to beautiful waterfalls and hill stations, Karnataka offers many places to explore.

Bengaluru is the capital city of Karnataka and is popularly known as the Silicon Valley of India.

Kannada is the official and most widely spoken language in the state of Karnataka

Popular Tourist Places

Mysuru

Mysuru is famous for Mysore Palace, Dasara celebrations and its rich cultural heritage.

Coorg

Coorg is a beautiful hill region known for coffee plantations, forests and pleasant weather.

Hampi

Hampi is famous for its ancient temples, monuments and ruins of the Vijayanagara Empire.

Gokarna

Gokarna is known for its beautiful beaches and peaceful surroundings.

Waterfalls & Hill Stations

Jog Falls

Jog Falls is one of the famous waterfalls of Karnataka and is surrounded by beautiful natural scenery.

Abbey Falls

Abbey Falls is a popular waterfall located near Madikeri in Coorg.

Chikmagalur

Chikmagalur is a popular hill station known for its coffee plantations and mountains.

Kudremukh

Kudremukh is known for its green mountains, forests and trekking routes.

Famous Karnataka Foods

Masala Dosa

A popular South Indian dish made with crispy dosa and potato filling.

Bisi Bele Bath

A traditional Karnataka dish prepared using rice, lentils and spices.

Mysore Pak

A famous sweet made using gram flour, ghee and sugar.

Ragi Mudde

A traditional Karnataka food prepared using ragi flour.

Travel Information

Best Time to Visit

Karnataka can be visited throughout the year. The cooler months are generally comfortable for sightseeing and outdoor activities.

Transportation

Karnataka has airports, railway stations and road transportation connecting major cities and tourist places.

Travel Tips

  • Carry sufficient water.
  • Keep important documents safe.
  • Follow local rules and instructions.
  • Keep tourist places clean.
  • Respect local culture and traditions.
